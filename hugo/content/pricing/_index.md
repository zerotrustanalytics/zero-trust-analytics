faq:
  - q: What counts as an event?
    a: "Every pageview and every custom event you send (like a signup or a download click) counts as one event. Your plan's allowance resets on the first of each month."
  - q: Is it really unlimited sites on every plan?
    a: "Yes, even on Free. Add as many sites as you like; plans are priced only on the total events across all of them."
  - q: What happens if I go over my allowance?
    a: "We let you know as you get close. If you go over, ZTA stops recording new events until the next month or until you upgrade. Your existing data and your sites are untouched."
  - q: Do I need a cookie banner for ZTA?
    a: "The ZTA script sets no cookies and writes nothing to your visitors' devices, and it never stores IP addresses. Whether your site needs a banner also depends on everything else it runs, so check with whoever advises you on privacy."
  - q: How long do you keep my data?
    a: "Individual events are kept for your plan's retention period, then deleted. Your charts and totals (the daily aggregates) are kept for as long as the site is in your account."
  - q: Can I cancel or export my data?
    a: "Any time, from the dashboard. You can export your data or delete it, and cancelling keeps you on Free rather than deleting your account."
  - q: Can I self-host instead?
    a: "Yes. ZTA runs as a single Docker container with a local database. See the self-hosting guide in the docs."
