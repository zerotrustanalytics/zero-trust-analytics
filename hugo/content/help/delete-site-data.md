Open the site, then **Settings** in its sidebar. **Site Information** shows the site's ID, domain and when it was added. Both tools are under **Danger Zone**.

![The site Danger Zone](/images/help/danger-zone.jpg)

## Delete a date range

Useful after a load test, a bot attack or a day of testing on production.

1. Under **Delete data by date range**, choose the **Start date** and **End date**.
2. Choose **Delete Range**.

## Purge all of a site's analytics

1. Under **Purge all analytics data**, type the site's domain to confirm.
2. Choose **Purge All Data**.

This permanently deletes the site's page views, totals and usage. The site itself stays, and keeps collecting new data. Neither action can be undone.
