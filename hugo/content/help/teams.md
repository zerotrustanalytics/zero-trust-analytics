## Create a team

1. Open **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **New Team**, enter a **Team Name**, and choose **Create Team**.

You're the team's **Owner**.

## Invite people

1. Pick the team under **Select Team**.
2. Choose **Invite Member**.
3. Enter their **Email Address** and pick a **Role**:
   - **Admin**: full access, including managing members.
   - **Editor**: can view and edit sites.
   - **Viewer**: read-only access.
4. Choose **Send Invitation**.

**Team Members** lists everyone with their role, status and when they joined.

How many people you can invite depends on your plan; see [Pricing](/pricing/).
