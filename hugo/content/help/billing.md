Open **Billing** in the sidebar.

- **Current Plan** shows your plan and when the billing period ends.
- **Usage This Month** shows events used against your plan's monthly allowance, your websites (unlimited on every plan) and your team members.
- **Available Plans** lists every plan. Choose **Upgrade** on the one you want.
- **Manage Billing** opens the billing portal, where you manage your payment details and subscription.

## What counts toward your allowance

Every page view and every custom event counts as one event. Your monthly total covers all your sites together. You'll see a warning as you approach the limit. See all plans and allowances on [Pricing](/pricing/).
