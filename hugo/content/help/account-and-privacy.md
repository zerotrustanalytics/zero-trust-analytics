Open **Account** in the sidebar.

## Sign-in and passkeys

Your account's email is shown under **Sign-in**. There is no password to change: you sign in with a passkey through gatekeep. Choose **Manage passkeys** to add a new device, or remove one you no longer use.

## White-label branding

For reports and shared dashboards with your own brand, choose **Configure Branding**:

1. Switch on **Enable Custom Branding**.
2. Enter your **Company Name** (shown in email report headers and shared dashboard footers).
3. Add a **Logo URL**. A 200×50px PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best.
4. Pick a **Primary Color** for buttons, links and accents in reports.
5. Choose **Save Branding Settings**.

Branding is available on some plans; see [Pricing](/pricing/).

## Download everything

Under **Your Data**, choose **Export my data**. You get a JSON file with your account details and your sites.

## Delete your account

Under **Danger Zone**, choose **Delete Account**, type your email to confirm, then **Delete everything**.

This immediately and permanently erases your account, every site and all of their analytics. There is no restore window, and nothing can bring it back. Export first if you might need the data.
