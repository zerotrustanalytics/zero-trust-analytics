## Export from Google Analytics 4

1. In GA4, go to **Reports** and open a report, such as *Pages and screens* or *Traffic acquisition*.
2. Set the date range you want.
3. Choose **Share** (top right), then **Download file → Download CSV**.

Pages, traffic sources, geography and device reports all work. The more of them you import, the richer your history.

## Import into ZTA

1. Open **Import Data** in the sidebar.
2. **Select Target Site**.
3. Drop the CSV (or JSON) file onto **Upload GA Export File**, or click it to choose the file.
