## Share a read-only link

1. Open the site, then choose **Share** in its sidebar.
2. On **Share Link**, pick a **Link Expiration**: 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or never.
3. Choose **Create Link** and send the link to whoever needs it.

![The Share Dashboard dialog](/images/help/share.jpg)

Anyone with the link can view that site's analytics, without an account, until the link expires or you revoke it. They can't change anything.

## Embed your stats on another page

Embedding builds on a share link. Create one first (above), then open the **Embed Code** tab in the same **Share** dialog and copy the snippet into your website or blog. If you haven't made a link yet, the tab offers **Create Share Link**.

## Download your data as CSV

Open the site and choose **Export** in its sidebar. A CSV file for the date range you're viewing downloads straight away, named after the site and range (for example `example.com-analytics-7d-2026-10-30.csv`). Pick a different range first if you need one.
