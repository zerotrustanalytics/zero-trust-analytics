---
title: "Page values"
description: "Put a money value on important pages to see return on your traffic without e-commerce."
weight: 7
---

Give a page a value, such as $500 for `/thank-you` after a lead form, and ZTA can show the value your traffic brings in.

1. Open the site, then **Page Values** in its sidebar.
2. Choose **Add Rule**.
3. Enter a **Rule Name** and the **Page Pattern**.
4. Choose a **Match Type**:
   - **Exact match**: the URL must match exactly.
   - **Contains**: the URL contains the text.
   - **Starts with**: the URL starts with the path.
   - **Regex**: a regular expression.
5. Enter the **Value** and pick the **Currency** (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD or AUD).
6. Leave **Enabled** on, and choose **Create**.
