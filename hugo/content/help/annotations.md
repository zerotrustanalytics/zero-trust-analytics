---
title: "Annotations"
description: "Mark launches, campaigns and incidents on your timeline so traffic changes make sense later."
weight: 5
---

A spike in March means more when you can see "Product Hunt launch" next to it.

1. Open **Annotations** in the sidebar.
2. **Select Site**.
3. Choose **Add Annotation**.
4. Fill in a **Title**, an optional **Description**, and the **Date** it happened.
5. Pick a **Category**: Release, Campaign, Incident or Other.
6. Choose **Add**.
