## The overview: all your sites at once

**Dashboard** in the sidebar shows every site together.

![The dashboard overview](/images/help/dashboard.jpg)

- **Date range**: choose **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days** at the top right. **Refresh** reloads the numbers.
- **Totals**: sites, visitors, page views, sessions, average bounce rate and average visit duration, for the chosen range.
- **Traffic chart**: **Combined** adds all sites into one line; **Per Site** draws one line per site so you can compare them.
- **Sites Performance**: one row per site with its current visitors, visitors, page views, bounce rate and average time. **View** opens that site.

## One site in detail

Open a site from **Sites** (**View Analytics**) or from the dashboard (**View**).

![A site's analytics page](/images/help/site-overview.jpg)

- **Current visitors**, under the site name, counts people on the site right now.
- **Headline numbers**: unique visitors, page views, views per visit, bounce rate, average time on site and total sessions. The small badge on each compares with the previous period of the same length.
- **Traffic Overview**: switch between **Visitors** and **Page Views**, or choose **Cumulative** to see the running total across the range.
- **Pages**: **Top Pages** (most viewed), **Entry Pages** (where visits start) and **Exit Pages** (where they end). **Load More** shows the rest.
- **Where visitors come from**: **Channels** groups traffic (referral, organic search, organic social…); **Sources** lists the referring sites.
- **Campaigns**: **UTM Sources**, **UTM Mediums** and **UTM Campaigns** show traffic from links tagged with `utm_` parameters.
- **Location**: **Countries**, **Regions** and **Cities**.
- **Technology**: **Browsers**, **Operating Systems** and the desktop/mobile split under **Devices**.

> ZTA never sets cookies or stores anything on your visitors' devices. A "visitor" is counted from a daily, salted hash that can't be reversed or followed across days, so numbers are close estimates, not identities. [How counting works](/docs/privacy-model/).

## Real-time

**Real-time** in the sidebar shows what is happening now: choose a site, and you get the visitors active right now, counts for the last 30 minutes and today, and a **Live Activity Feed** of recent page views with each one's page, source and device type.
