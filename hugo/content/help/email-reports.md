---
title: "Email reports"
description: "Get a summary of a site's traffic in your inbox every day, week or month."
weight: 4
---

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Create Report**.
3. Pick the **Site**.
4. Pick the **Frequency**: daily (sent at 9am), weekly (Monday 9am) or monthly (the 1st).
5. Add the **Recipients**. Separate several addresses with commas.
6. Choose **Create Report**.

![Creating an email report](/images/help/email-report.jpg)

The **Reports** page also shows a preview of the email: visitors, page views and bounce rate, each compared with the previous period, with **View Full Report** linking to the site's analytics.
