Three steps: sign in, add your site, paste one line of code.

## 1. Sign in with a passkey

Go to [app.ztas.io](https://app.ztas.io/sign-in) and choose **Continue with passkey**. ZTA uses passkeys, so there is no password to create or remember. Your browser or phone (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key) confirms it's you.

New here? **Create an account** on the same screen. It uses the same passkey flow and starts you on the free plan with unlimited sites.

## 2. Add your website

1. In the sidebar, open **Sites**.
2. Choose **Add Site**.
3. Enter the **Domain** without `http://` or `https://` (for example `example.com`).
4. Optionally give it a **Site Name** so you can tell sites apart.
5. Choose **Add Site**.

![The Add New Site dialog](/images/help/add-site.jpg)

## 3. Install the tracking code

On the **Sites** page, choose **Copy Tracking Code** on your site's card. The button changes to **Copied!**. Paste the code into your site's HTML, just before `</head>`, on every page.

For WordPress, Next.js, Hugo and other setups, follow the [installation guide](/docs/installation/).

## 4. Check it's working

Visit your website, then open **Real-time** in the sidebar and choose your site. Your visit should appear within a few seconds. Then head to [Read your dashboard](/help/your-dashboard/).
