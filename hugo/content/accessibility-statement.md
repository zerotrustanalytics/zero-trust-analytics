{{< param "legalName" >}} wants ztas.io and the Zero Trust Analytics dashboard to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology.

## Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

## Current status

We have not yet completed a formal audit. Today:

- Text and interface colors are chosen to meet WCAG AA contrast, in light and dark mode.
- Pages can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator, and include a "skip to main content" link.
- Animations stop when your device asks for reduced motion.
- Decorative illustrations are hidden from screen readers; meaningful ones have text descriptions.

The tracking script adds no visible content to our customers' websites and does not affect their accessibility.

## Feedback

If you hit a barrier, [tell us](/contact/) which page and what went wrong. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
