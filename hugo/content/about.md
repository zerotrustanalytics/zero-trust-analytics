Zero Trust is a security idea: never trust, always verify. Don't assume anything inside the wall is safe; design as if someone is already looking.

We applied that to web analytics, starting with ourselves. Most analytics tools collect everything and ask you to trust that it'll be handled well. ZTA is designed so there's nothing sensitive to handle: if we never store it, nobody can leak it, sell it or subpoena it.

## What that means in practice

- **No cookies, no device storage.** Nothing is written to your visitors' browsers, so the script needs no consent banner of its own.
- **No IP addresses on file.** Visitors are counted with a one-way hash that resets every day.
- **No cross-site tracking.** Each site's data is separate, and a visitor can't be followed from one site to another.
- **Data minimisation.** Page paths without query strings, referring domains, campaign tags, approximate location and device type. [That's the whole list.](/docs/data-collected/)

## Why it matters

You can understand what's working on your website without building a profile of every person who visits it. Your visitors keep their privacy, you keep the numbers that matter, and when privacy rules tighten you have nothing to unwind.
